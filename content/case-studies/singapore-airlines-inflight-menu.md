## Problem

The menu family needed to work across five cabin classes and accommodate different formats, routes, and premium-service expectations.

## Delivery

The proposed covers use close-up photography of fresh produce to reflect Singapore Airlines’ meticulous approach to food and service. Business Class and above use a booklet format that accepts special menu inserts; Economy and Premium Economy use a brochure layout designed for outbound and return routes.
