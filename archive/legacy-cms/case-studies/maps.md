## Overview

Maps helps insurance assistance operators find onward mobility after a client's vehicle can no longer continue. It focuses on searching for and booking transport; the assistance case itself is managed in a separate product.

My role included translating business and UX needs into user stories, designing the interface and design system, facilitating workshops, managing stakeholders and collaborating with development.

## Problem

During a live call, an operator must turn incomplete circumstances into a feasible journey. A solution may combine train, rental car and taxi, including taxi connections—called *liaisons*—between the client's location, transport hubs and destination.

## Process

### Persona — Insurance assistance operator

This role persona is based on the operational workflow rather than demographic assumptions. The operator gathers journey constraints, compares available modes and explains the selected solution while speaking with a potentially stressed client.

The case study focuses on three design decisions: capturing complex requirements, comparing recommendations and making a multimodal journey understandable.

## Solution

The flow starts with structured search fields for the journey, passengers and special requirements. Results compare train, rental-car and taxi options using consistent criteria. Each recommendation reveals its connections and transfers, including required taxi liaisons, before the operator continues through booking.
