## Overview

assisteur supports the wider assistance workflow. Unlike Maps, it is a case-management system: operators create and manage a client case, then create a mission for each service that needs to be arranged.

## Problem

Resolving a case is not always a single search-and-book action. An operator may arrange replacement mobility, then return later to organise taxi liaisons. The product must preserve context and make outstanding work visible over time.

## Process

### Persona — Insurance assistance operator

This persona shares a job title with the Maps persona but works in a different context. Here the operator owns the continuity of the case: recording what happened, creating missions, tracking their states and returning for dependent services. They need orchestration and traceability more than side-by-side journey comparison.

## Solution

The flow begins with creating or opening a client case. The operator creates a mission for each required service, arranges replacement mobility and later adds taxi liaisons where necessary. The interface makes the relationship between the case, its missions and remaining work explicit.
